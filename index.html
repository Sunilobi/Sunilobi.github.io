How I approach operations

Investigate. Stabilize.
Prevent recurrence.

A practical incident-to-learning loop used to turn recurring symptoms into clearer troubleshooting and more durable operational knowledge.

STEP 01

Detect & scope

Use alerts, events, logs and service-health signals to establish impact and isolate the failing layer.

STEP 02

Investigate

Trace dependencies across pods, nodes, networking, ingress, credentials and supporting services.

STEP 03

Recover & verify

Coordinate safe remediation, validate platform health and confirm the service path is working.

STEP 04

Learn & document

Record root cause, corrective/preventive actions and a reusable runbook or knowledge article.
